About

Built on real markets.
Proven across continents.

Njenga founded Golden Pipit Recruiting after building a search practice that spans some of the world's most demanding hiring markets. He has sourced and placed talent across Latin America, East Africa, Europe, and Asia — working directly with founders, CEOs, and boards to meet their most critical people needs.

His client list spans the full spectrum of company maturity — from early-stage startups finding their first senior hires, to growth-stage companies scaling rapidly across new markets, to established regional and multinational firms replacing key leadership. What stays constant is the approach: rigorous, relationship-led, and anchored in a genuine understanding of what each business actually needs.

Njenga has a particular edge in cross-border search — identifying talent in markets where most recruiters don't operate, and navigating the cultural and commercial nuances that make international placements succeed. His network is not a database. It is a living set of relationships built over years of active search on multiple continents.
