# Golden Pipit Recruiting — Website v2

Development branch: `website-v2`. The existing `main` site is unchanged.

## Direction
Employer-facing recruitment for organisations filling roles as needed, across functions and seniority levels. Executive search is one proof point, not the entire proposition.

## Homepage architecture
1. Navigation: Services / How we work / Client stories / About / Contact
2. Hero: **The right people. For the roles that matter.** Subheading: Practical, attentive recruiting support for organisations building stronger teams. Primary CTA: **Tell us who you're hiring**.
3. Service cards: Role-based recruitment; specialist and leadership hiring; talent mapping and market insight.
4. Process: Understand the brief / Search and assess / Present a thoughtful shortlist / Support the hire.
5. Proof: Reuse real testimonials from the existing `index.html` verbatim, with their original attribution.
6. About: recruiter-led, hands-on delivery.
7. Enquiry: role, location, hiring timeline, contact details.
8. Footer: distinct link to Pipit Career Services and Golden Pipit Solutions.

## Visual system
Untitled UI-inspired spacing, responsive cards, clean typography. Existing site source uses Inter, navy #0F1C2E and gold #F5A623. Retain brand recognition while refining hierarchy and accessibility.

## Editorial rules
No invented testimonials, client logos, success statistics or partnership claims. Keep candidate-facing career services separate from the employer enquiry funnel. Do not deploy or replace `index.html` without review.
